Safety Functions

The OHLEC Process

  1. Objective: What is the goal of the cut? (e.g., bucking, directional felling).
  2. Hazards: Identifying dangers (overhead, ground, bystanders).
  3. Leans & Binds: Analyzing tension and compression in the wood.
  4. Escape Plan: Planning a safe retreat path before starting the saw.
  5. Cut Plan: Deciding the exact sequence of cuts based on the binds.

Controls & Help

Use mouse drag to Orbit/Pan.

Tools: Press 1 for Wedge, 2 for Hand, Escape to deselect.

Chainsaw Move: W/S/A/D Translate, Q/E Yaw, Z/C Pitch.

Chainsaw Actions: T Toggle Throttle, R Reset Position.

Chainsaw Snapping: Arrow Keys to snap/flip position.

Gizmo: M to toggle Translate/Rotate mode.

System: Press F for Debug Tools, H to toggle Help.
